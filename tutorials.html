Basics

There are two game Launchers that hold all of the games. You could compare this to a digital bookshelf but for games.

These launchers are Steam and Epic Games Launcher.
Every game in our library is individually bought or obtained for free on these platforms.

Different games are on different platforms. You must download and install the launcher that matches the game(s) you want to play.

The launchers wont take a large amount of space unless games are downloaded, so feel free to install both.

Free Games / Playing Together

Games that are free can be played on the house account, but you most likely would enjoy them more by playing them on your own account. This way, you can keep your personal progress no matter where you play.

Additionally, if you want to play with someone in the house you cannot both play on house account, so making your own is necessary to play something multiplayer (free or not) like Marvel Rivals together.

Create Steam Account | Create Epic Games Account

If you use the house computers, just remember to log out of your personal account when you are done playing.

Downloading Games

Once you are logged in, click on the "Library" tab at the left or top of the screen.

You can use the search bar to find specific games, or you can just browse the library to find something to play.

When you find what you want, press it then press "Install" to download it to your computer.

Once the download has finished, you can click the game then launch to play it.

Uninstalling Games

To uninstall a game, right click it in the library then click on "Uninstall", or hover over "Manage", then click "Uninstall".
